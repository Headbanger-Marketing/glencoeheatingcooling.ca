Your Local Middlesex County Crew

Built for Southwest Middlesex Farm Country, Not the City

Glencoe Heating & Cooling is a local HVAC company based on Main Street in Glencoe, serving the village and the surrounding farms of Southwest Middlesex. We work on the propane and oil furnaces that actually heat homes out here, not just the gas units most city crews are built around.

Natural gas barely reaches past Mount Brydges, so our day-to-day is propane furnaces, legacy oil units in century farmhouses, and the cold-climate heat pumps more farmers are switching to every year. We pair those systems with Generac standby generators because ice storms knock the grid out here for days at a time, and a farmhouse with no heat in January is an emergency, plain and simple.

Backed by our 100% satisfaction guarantee on every job.

FAQs

Heating & Cooling Questions From Around Glencoe

Answers to the questions we hear most from homeowners across Glencoe, Newbury, Mount Brydges, Wardsville, Melbourne, and Appin.

Do you work on propane and oil furnaces, or just gas?

Most homes around Glencoe run propane or oil, not gas, so propane and oil furnaces are most of what we do. We service legacy oil units in century farmhouses, high-efficiency propane furnaces in newer builds, and the cold-climate heat pumps more farmers are switching to every year.

How fast can you reach a farmhouse outside Glencoe for a no-heat call?

We are based on Main Street in Glencoe, so most calls across the village and out to Wardsville, Melbourne, Appin, and Newbury are a short drive. No-heat calls in winter get priority, and a local Middlesex County technician heads out fast.

Can you pair a Generac generator with my furnace or heat pump?

Yes. As a Generac dealer we size and install standby generators that keep your propane furnace, heat pump, well pump, and AC running through extended outages. Ice storms can knock the grid out here for days, and backup power is how we solve that.

Do you handle oil-to-heat-pump conversions?

Yes. We remove the old oil furnace, size a cold-climate heat pump to carry the full heating load through January, handle the rebate paperwork, and can pair the system with Generac backup so your farmhouse never freezes during an outage.

Are you licensed and insured for HVAC work in Ontario?

Yes. Glencoe Heating & Cooling is a fully licensed and insured HVAC company serving Southwest Middlesex, with certified technicians who work on all major furnace, AC, and heat-pump brands common to the area.

What does a service call or quote cost?

Quotes are free and carry no obligation. Before any work starts, we explain what is wrong and what it will cost, in plain language, so you can decide with the full picture. No surprise add-ons, ever.
